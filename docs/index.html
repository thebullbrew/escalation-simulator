7How escalation clauses actually work

  • You name three numbers: your base offer, the increment you escalate by, and your cap. If a bona fide competing offer beats your base, your offer automatically climbs past it by your increment — up to your cap.
  • The seller will want proof that the competing offer exists. No phantom bids — your agent should expect the listing agent to ask for documentation.
  • Some sellers refuse escalation clauses outright. Ask the listing agent before you write one, or your clever clause dies unread.
  • Your cap is visible to the seller. In a thin field, you've just shown them your ceiling. That's the price of the weapon.
  • Never escalate past what the house will appraise at unless you have the cash to cover the gap. The bank won't lend on your optimism.
